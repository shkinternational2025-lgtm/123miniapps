Before you write

A few things that resolve most incoming questions faster than an email would:

  • A tool isn't working. Try a hard refresh first, an outdated cached service worker is the usual culprit. If that fails, tell us your browser and version.
  • A tool is not behaving as expected. All 95 tools are live, each with its own page. Tell us which one and what you expected, the more specific, the faster it gets fixed.
  • Is my data really private? Yes, and you can verify it, open DevTools, watch the Network tab while using any tool, and you will see nothing carrying your input. See the privacy policy for detail.
  • Can I use output commercially? Yes, with no attribution required. See the terms.

Reporting accessibility problems

Accessibility issues are treated as bugs, not enhancement requests. If something is unreachable by keyboard, unreadable by a screen reader, or fails contrast in one of the nine themes, please report it and include the theme name and the assistive technology you were using.

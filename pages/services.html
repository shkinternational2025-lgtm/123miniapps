          FAQ
          
Questions, answered.

          
Anything else? Email me and you will get a straight answer, usually within a day.

        

        

          
What does SHK International build?
AI-powered websites and web apps, custom browser tools and full tool websites, self-hosted n8n automations, small bots and AI assistants, digital products, SEO article writing, VPS setup and hosting, and original AI music production.

          
How much does a project cost?
It depends on the scope. Tell me what you need and I will reply with a clear, fixed plan and an honest quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

          
How long does a typical project take?
A landing page or a single tool is usually a few days. A full multi-page site, an automation, or a bot is typically one to three weeks depending on complexity. I share a timeline with the plan.

          
Do I own the work and the code?
Yes. Everything I build is yours to keep, host and change. I hand over the files and, where relevant, set it up on your own server or hosting.

          
Can you work with my existing website or tools?
Yes. I can extend, fix or redesign an existing site, connect your current apps with automations, or add new tools and pages to what you already run.

          
Can I get original AI music for my project?
Yes. I produce original, release-ready AI music for brands, videos and catalogues, the same production I use for my own Synthetic Polyglot releases. Get in touch with what you need.
